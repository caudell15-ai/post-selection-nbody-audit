# Known unrecovered provenance

The surviving archive preserves:
- all 90 realized candidate states;
- bulk corridor bounds;
- hard constraints;
- heuristic score;
- later common-model validation history.

The archive does **not** presently support a reliable reconstruction of:
- the exact numerical angular-generation bounds for the original 90-candidate corridor;
- the original RNG seed for that corridor;
- whether the 90-candidate set was generated in a single-pass draw/grid or through adaptive refinement.

The manuscript explicitly discloses these limits. They must not be reconstructed from hindsight.
